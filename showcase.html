Example of Good and bad websites

Good Website: Niblu
  • This is an example of a good website.
  • It has a clean layout with a color scheme that makes the text easy to read.
  • The navigation bar is easy to find and use and the site is responsive
Bad Website: ARNGREN
  • This is an example of a bad website.
  • It has a cluttered layout and the header and footer are not properly defined.
  • Most of the content extends past the boundaries of the page and scrolling to the side is needed for it to be viewed.
  • Content of the site is not properly sectioned and the site is not responsive.
