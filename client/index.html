What is Mindfulness?

Mindfulness is a quality that every human being already possesses — "presence". This presence is to be had in every moment, living and existing in your body, environment, and mind. Mindfulness is especially helpful in challenging situations, because it reminds you of choice: to decide and act with clarity, over anxiety and confusion.

— Definition adapted from Headspace

Mindfulness has many forms, and can be created through:

  • Meditation — Seated, or Yoga
  • Taking short reflective pauses
  • Self-compassion
  • Realizing your power over your thoughts
  • Simply appreciating the moment

Our Purpose.

The McMaster Mindfulness Club aims to create a safe(r), brave, and relaxing space for students to develop deep appreciation, awareness and acceptance around one's mental health, emotions, and bodily sensations while living in the moment. This will be done through meditation practices, yoga, arts and crafts, reflection activities and various safe relaxation methods — all in the pursuit of experiencing mindfulness!

Founded in 2020, the MMC is dedicated to promoting mindfulness practices and maintaining self-wellbeing. Through online and in-person initiatives, events, and student outreach, the MMC is committed to acting as the central hub for mindfulness on campus — informing and educating students, providing meditative practices, and alleviating misinformation associated with mindfulness.
