Collaboration

Meditative Yoga: MacRehab × McMaster Mindfulness

MacRehab Club and McMaster Mindfulness came together to host a lovely meditative yoga session, blending rehabilitation science with mindful movement for the McMaster community.

Workshop · April 2022

Mindfulness and Creativity

In collaboration with Open Circle McMaster, this online interactive workshop explored mindfulness through creative expression — drawing, painting, and speech — led by Expressive Arts facilitator Marybeth Leis Druery. Attendees created mandalas as a focus for meditation.

Open Circle McMaster Marybeth Leis Druery
Speaker Event · November 2021

Mindfulness with Dr. JP Pawliw-Fry

Dr. JP Pawliw-Fry — co-author of Performing Under Pressure and creator of The Last 8% Podcast — hosted an online mindfulness event sharing breathing meditations and his ETA Tool (Explore, Tenderness, Acceptance) for managing difficult emotions.

Workshop · March 2021

Mindful Self-Compassion

Registered psychotherapist Sally Dwyer led a Mindful Self-Compassion workshop exploring how MSC reduces depression, stress, and anxiety while enhancing happiness and optimism. Participants engaged in experimental practices and discussions on burnout and empathy fatigue.
